## Rules

1. One identifier represents one question.
2. Questions must be concrete enough to obtain a definitive answer.
3. Hypotheses are not validated business rules.
4. `BLOCKING` questions prevent approval of affected specs or plans.
5. When resolved, update the appropriate canonical document and reference it here.
6. Keep resolved questions for traceability; do not delete their history.
