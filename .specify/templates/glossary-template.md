## Rules

1. One business concept SHOULD have one canonical term.
2. Definitions describe business meaning, not implementation.
3. Avoid framework, database, API, or UI terminology.
4. If two terms have different business meanings, define them separately.
5. Do not silently redefine an established term; update the glossary and affected normative documents.
6. Add only terms whose meaning matters to understanding the domain.
